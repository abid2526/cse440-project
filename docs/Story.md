# Story Design

## Story Background

The kingdom's most valuable treasure, the Ancient Crystal, has been hidden inside a mysterious cave for many years. Legends say that the crystal has the power to bring peace and prosperity to the kingdom.

The player takes the role of an adventurer who sets out on a journey to find the Ancient Crystal. Along the way, the player will explore different locations, interact with characters, collect useful items, and face various challenges.

Every important decision made by the player affects future events, making each playthrough slightly different.

---

## Main Objective

The player's goal is to find the Ancient Crystal and safely complete the adventure.

Depending on the player's choices and current condition, the journey may end successfully or unsuccessfully.

---

## Game Locations

### 1. Village

This is the starting location of the game.

Possible events:

- Meet the Merchant
- Talk to the Old Man
- Rest at the Inn

---

### 2. Forest

The player travels through the forest on the way to the cave.

Possible events:

- Wolf Attack
- Hidden Treasure
- Lost Traveler

---

### 3. Cave

The cave contains the final challenges.

Possible events:

- Goblin Battle
- Find the Ancient Crystal
- Final Guardian

---

## Player Variables

The game will keep track of the following player information:

- Health
- Gold
- Weapon
- Reputation
- Current Location

These variables will be updated throughout the game and will influence future events.

---

## Possible Endings

### Good Ending

The player finds the Ancient Crystal and safely completes the adventure.

---

### Neutral Ending

The player finds the crystal but survives with low health or loses most of their resources.

---

### Bad Ending

The player loses all health before completing the mission.

---

## Dynamic Storytelling

Unlike a traditional adventure game, the story does not always follow the same path.

The player's current state and previous decisions influence which events occur next. This allows different players to experience different storylines even though the main objective remains the same.
